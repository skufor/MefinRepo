## Current policy

MefinRepo will not mirror a third-party package merely because it is downloadable from another repository.

For each package, one of the following should be available before mirroring:

1. explicit redistribution permission from the developer/rightsholder; or
2. a package/source license that clearly permits redistribution of the binary, with required notices preserved.

Paid packages and packages whose terms do not grant redistribution rights will remain external references rather than being copied into MefinRepo.
